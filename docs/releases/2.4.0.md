# CathoScroll 2.4.0 — native release notes

Status: prepared on PR #22; not yet released.

## Feed and recommendations

- Adds the redesigned ordered feed with topic, Top Comments and sponsor cards registered as independent feed entries.
- Keeps the online general feed server-ranked using shared site-wide engagement aggregates.
- Android and Electron cache the latest server-ranked general-feed pages for offline use. Cached pages preserve the last server-computed order; they do not recalculate global ranking while offline.
- Android and Electron can continue through previously downloaded personalised feed pages while the last successful online consent check is no more than 12 hours old. Personalised page order is stored in the encrypted private database, scoped to the signed-in account, and cleared on consent withdrawal or account change. No new interest profile is inferred offline.
- If the personalised cache is unavailable or its consent check expires, the app falls back to cached general ranking, then catalogue order.

## Offline analytics

- Signed-out topic/comment analytics use a separate anonymous SQLite outbox in the native catalogue database, capped at 2,000 events and seven days, with batches of 40 and server-receipt deduplication.
- Signed-in topic and Top Comments analytics use an account-scoped outbox in the encrypted private activity database on Android and Electron, capped at 2,000 events per stream and seven days. Rows are deleted only after server acknowledgement and are cleared on sign-out/account change.
- Web retry queues remain in memory. Sponsor analytics remain a separate pipeline.
- Likes, bookmarks and completion continue to use the existing revision-aware activity sync. Analytics delivery does not replace or modify that sync.

## Notes and privacy

- Notes remain encrypted and available for offline reading where supported. Editing remains online-only; no offline note-write queue is introduced.
- The signed-out analytics opt-out clears pending anonymous events.
- Native analytics storage and the bounded personalised page cache are documented in the Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy. The controller must complete the applicable PECR terminal-equipment assessment before distribution.
